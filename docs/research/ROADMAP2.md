# Roadmap 2 — admitted-corpus correlation and temporal standing

**Status:** parked research incubator; non-authorizing; not a governed execution
lane. Captured 2026-07-16.

This document gives stable names to a possible post-V0 research program:
compile exact admitted post versions into explainable discussion clusters, then
project how much temporal standing those clusters have for one community.

It is called Roadmap 2 because that is a useful human handle, not because it is
the project's second authorization ledger or its next sprint. The canonical
candidate ladder remains [ROADMAP.md](../../ROADMAP.md). The sole authorizing
document remains [BUILD.md](../../BUILD.md).

> A post is an observation. A cluster is a correlation hypothesis. Temporal
> standing is a community-local view annotation. None of the three is a
> verdict, an admission, or authority.

## The research proposition

Social feeds present documents as a stream. Repetition makes old information
feel current and makes one evidentiary origin look like many confirmations.
This program tests a different projection:

~~~text
exact admitted post versions
  -> structural relationships
  -> typed discussion clusters
  -> origin and amplification structure
  -> community-local temporal standing as of a declared time
  -> an explainable AppView lens
~~~

The admission ledger is what keeps the experiment bounded. The first question
is not “which posts in the Atmosphere describe the same real-world event?” It
is:

> Within one community's exactly admitted corpus, does a deterministic
> correlation view make repeated discussion more coherent without pretending
> to know whether a claim is true?

That is a smaller and more testable object than event detection. It also
preserves the current architecture:

~~~text
communityd
  writes only already-ratified community authority records
  does not read cluster, standing, observer, or archive state

communitywatch
  verifies and retains exact admitted observations
  derives anchors, cluster snapshots, provenance structure, and view state
  remains detect-only and unable to sign or write ATProto records

community AppView
  is the exact, explainable product surface

optional communityfeed
  is a later URI-only compatibility peephole for stock clients
  cannot impersonate the exact AppView
~~~

No new writer, authority record, label emission, or modification of PDS
behavior belongs in the initial program.

## Constitutional laws

These laws bind every candidate in this file and must migrate with any future
slice.

1. **Admission is scope, not endorsement.** A valid admission records the
   community actor's acceptance of an exact post version. Current rendering
   still depends on removal, terminal CID, and availability state. Admission
   does not ratify the post's claims.
2. **The post is not the claim or event.** V0 has posts and admission records,
   not a canonical claim identity. An initial cluster therefore describes
   discussion structure, not a real-world event.
3. **A cluster is not a verdict.** Shared URLs, reply roots, quoted records, or
   media CIDs show a relationship. They do not establish truth, falsity,
   coordination, causation, or common intent.
4. **Volume is not corroboration.** Where authenticated record relationships
   show one source amplified one hundred times, the result remains one
   structural origin group plus known amplifications. One hundred posts
   sharing only a URL are one URL-anchor group and one hundred occurrences;
   their independence and amplification relations remain unknown. Different
   DIDs do not, by themselves, prove independent witnesses.
5. **Freshness is not truth.** A stale observation need not be false. An active
   observation need not be correct.
6. **Temporal standing is view policy only.** The schema and UI term is
   **discussion standing**. It is keyed by community, cluster snapshot, policy
   version, and as-of time. It never grants governance standing or changes
   admission, membership, role, removal, moderation, or ranking authority.
7. **History is append-only.** Removal, CID mismatch, a later update, or a new
   projection changes current rendering; it does not rewrite an earlier exact
   observation or cluster snapshot.
8. **Exact versions remain exact.** Canonical evidence carries both AT URI and
   CID. A mutable URI is never treated as equivalent to the admitted record
   version.
9. **The canonical chronological view remains available.** The first
   clustered surface is an optional lens, not a replacement or silent default.
10. **No scalar collapse.** No truth score, community-confidence score,
    virality score, contributor score, trending score, or composite standing
    number may substitute for named evidence and refusal states.
11. **Plurality survives inconvenience.** Incompatible observations may
    remain incompatible. The compiler may refuse to merge, type, refresh, or
    resolve them.
12. **Derived products expose their joins.** Cluster membership, coverage,
    time basis, algorithm and policy versions, missing evidence, and
    cannot-testify statements are inspectable.
13. **Derived state is rebuildable or marked otherwise.** Every result pins
    its input cutoff and evidence. Missing historical bytes yield
    cannot-replay-exactly, never invented testimony.
14. **Observer and emitter remain disjoint.** Communitywatch cannot acquire
    writer credentials or dependencies. Communityd cannot read observer
    storage, cluster state, AppView receipts, Parquet, or DuckDB.
15. **No label carries topology or standing.** Labels may remain presentation
    hints under existing doctrine. They cannot encode cluster membership,
    discussion standing, resolution, admission, or community authority.
16. **The clustered lens cannot expand eligibility.** At the same as-of,
    input cutoff, and canonical eligibility generation, every exact URI+CID
    exposed through a current, exact-capable R2 community-AppView surface is
    renderable in the then-ratified canonical chronological projection.
    Historical audit artifacts remain separately typed and non-renderable.
    R2 consumes that canonical projection; it cannot independently reinterpret
    admission validity, removal, CID terminality, current eligibility, or
    chronological order. A URI-only stock-feed skeleton is a separately typed,
    disclosed lossy adapter: its URI must have been selected from an eligible
    exact reference in the pinned generation, but downstream hydration is not
    an exact-CID or subset-conformance claim.
17. **Mechanical assurance is scoped testimony.** A proof, model check,
    executable specification, type boundary, or passing test establishes only
    its named property under pinned assumptions. It does not authenticate
    evidence, settle human policy, prove runtime conformance, promote a
    candidate, select an operator answer, or authorize work.

The assurance record keeps these states separate:

~~~text

  != abstract artifact checked
  != contract adopted
  != implementation mapped
  != runtime correspondence tested
  != slice accepted or authorized
~~~

Every applicable law or refusal is classified as machine-checkable,
human/governance-only, or explicitly unmodeled. Machine claims pin admitted
inputs, excluded perturbations, operating regime, assumptions, positive
witnesses, named tempting implementations, countermodels, and exact
model-to-runtime mappings. `Unmodeled` is an open finding, not a pass.

R2 must consume a read-only generation from the then-ratified canonical
eligibility rules, not build a second admission/removal/CID reducer. The
[V0 state machine](../V0-STATE-MACHINE.md) is the baseline; a migrated

cutoff and as-of:

~~~text
current exact-capable R2 AppView versions
  ⊆ then-canonical renderable exact versions
~~~

Disabling the alternate lens and erasing R2 fields leaves the chronological
projection unchanged. A lossy alternate surface may omit or group eligible
items; it may never add an ineligible item or alter authority state. The
URI-only stock-feed adapter instead proves only selection from an eligible
exact reference at its pinned generation, suppresses a mismatch known before
response, and discloses downstream hydration and race behavior outside the
exact-subset guarantee.

## Smallest honest experiment

The minimum coherent research program is R2-C1 through R2-C5:

- one community;
- retained exact admitted post versions, initially from offline fixtures or
  already-authorized observer custody;
- deterministic URL, reply-root, record-reference, and exact-media anchors;
- typed, overlapping discussion clusters rather than a global event
  partition;
- structural origin groups and amplification relations, with no independence
  or corroboration claim;
- one generic discussion-standing policy with explicit as-of time;
- only active, cooling, stale, and unknown states;
- a side-by-side chronological and clustered AppView;
- complete explanations of why each post was grouped and why each state was
  assigned;
- no semantic event extraction, automatic title generation, typed phenomenon
  classifier, locality inference, resolution verdict, emitted label, or stock
  feed requirement.

R2-C6 is a compatibility experiment. R2-C7 through R2-C9 are frontier
research. This is therefore a sequence of possible evidence-gated slices, not
one “sprint two” bundle.

## Data model sketch

These names reserve semantics, not an implementation schema.

### Exact admitted observation events and current projection

Each immutable verification event must distinguish at least:

- community DID and local admission lifecycle;
- exact submission, admission, and subject strong references;
- subject AT URI and CID;
- retained record bytes, or a durable evidence URI plus media type, byte
  length, digest, verification method, and digest-verified readback policy;
- source-authored time as an attributed field, not trusted network order;
- admission-record time;
- one verified observer time;
- local monotonic observer sequence or equivalent custody coordinate; and
- source delivery, authenticity, gap, quarantine, and refusal facts known for
  that event.

A separate generation-scoped projection derives first/last verified times,
current coverage, lifecycle state, and render eligibility as of an explicit
time. Those summaries never mutate an observation event.

If exact record evidence was not retained, the compiler must not reconstruct
it from the current record at the same URI. AT repositories do not provide a
canonical historical record archive.

The derived graph separates one deduplicated exact-subject content node keyed
by URI+CID from lifecycle-scoped admission-binding edges. Several valid
admission records for the same exact subject remain separate audit events and
bindings but cannot inflate content-member, origin, or amplification counts.
A later valid lifecycle may make the content node currently renderable through
a new binding; it never revives an old binding, reuses its state, or treats a
new admission time as fresh evidence. A newly admitted CID at the same URI is
a distinct content node and admission lifecycle.

### Anchor observation

An anchor observation records:

- exact member URI and CID;
- one typed anchor kind;
- raw and normalized values;
- extractor and normalization versions;
- evidence location or digest;
- extraction time and input high-water;
- admissibility or a typed refusal.

Provisional first hard-anchor kinds, only as selected and bounded by R2-Q9,
are:

- canonical external URL;
- explicit reply root;
- explicit quoted or embedded record strong reference;
- exact media blob CID.

Near-duplicate text, headlines, perceptual hashes, embeddings, inferred
locations, and inferred real-world entities are not initial hard anchors.
Native repost records are not V0 admitted post subjects. Repost ancestry may
appear later only as authenticated, non-member context under an explicitly
wider R2-Q1 boundary; it is not an admitted-corpus hard anchor by default.
The initial extractor reads anchor values from retained admitted record
evidence; it does not fetch external URLs or download media.

### Typed cluster series and snapshot

A post may belong to several typed clusters. A URL cluster and a reply-root
cluster do not become one alleged event merely because one bridge post belongs
to both.

A compiler output distinguishes:

- a **series key**, scoped to community, anchor kind, normalized anchor, and
  algorithm namespace;
- a **generation**, scoped to an exact input cutoff, compiler version, and
  canonicalization manifest; and
- a **snapshot ID**, derived from the generation, typed series key, sorted
  exact members, and edge-evidence digests.

Series keys are correlation handles, not durable real-world event identities.
Algorithm changes, normalization changes, merges, or splits create new
snapshots with explicit predecessor relations. They never rewrite old
membership or imply that two snapshots are ontologically the same event.

### Origin and amplification projection

The projection may describe only what the retained graph supports:

- source-origin group;
- direct record reference;
- authenticated repost context or quotation;
- reply participation;
- same external-source anchor;
- amplification with relation known;
- relationship unknown.

It may publish counts such as exact members, renderable members, origin groups,
and known amplifications. It may not rename origin-group count as independent
confirmation or corroboration. Unknown coverage remains unknown rather than
zero.

Raw occurrence custody and effective origin support are separate objects. A
raw occurrence means a distinct exact record or semantic variant, or a retained
source receipt required by the selected R2-Q10 custody policy; it does not
require retaining every byte-identical transport retry. The raw ledger orders
what it retains only by a named observer-local sequence/custody coordinate and
preserves lifecycle/variant and replay evidence at that declared fidelity.
Payload expiry leaves the declared tombstone and replay limitation.
The effective projection deduplicates only under a declared, versioned origin
key and is idempotent under replay and batch/stream decomposition. Remembering
a custody-policy-required duplicate/replay receipt must not increase effective
support.

Origin-key equality says nothing about payload fidelity. Different payloads or
claims under one proposed key remain attributed variants with an explicit
conflict/unknown result or refuse; they are never silently merged. Conversely,
equal effective support cannot reconstruct payload, authenticate an origin,
prove common-cause independence, or exclude Sybils.

### Community cluster state

A prospective state row needs at least:

- community DID;
- cluster snapshot ID and generation;
- discussion-standing state;
- explicit as-of time;
- policy ID, version, and digest;
- projection producer identity;
- policy ratifier principal and declared role;
- policy effective time, superseded-policy reference, and withdrawal state;
- the qualifying evidence time and its basis;
- last discussion activity time, kept separate from qualifying evidence;
- relevant source and observation high-waters;
- coverage/refusal state;
- computed-at time; and
- explanation-receipt digest.

Discussion activity does not automatically refresh evidentiary standing. A
three-day-old screenshot reposted today may make discussion current while the
underlying observation remains stale.

“Qualifying evidence” is not implementation discretion. A ratified view-policy
artifact must enumerate the admissible relation classes, select the attributed
clock for each class, define thresholds, define conflicting/missing-time
behavior, and say which relations—if any—refresh standing. Unless a named
admissible relation supplies its declared clock, the state is unknown.

## Time and replay discipline

The system has no single innocent “timestamp.” At minimum it must keep:

| Clock | What it can testify to | What it cannot testify to |
|---|---|---|
| source-authored time | what the record asserted | true event time or network order |
| first verified observation | when this observer verified an exact version | when the record first existed everywhere |
| admission time | when community authority admitted the exact version | when the described event occurred |
| relation activity time | when related discussion was observed | fresh independent evidence |
| computation time | when this projection ran | freshness of the underlying evidence |
| explicit as-of time | the replay question being answered | completeness beyond the pinned input window |

Every time-dependent result must take as-of as an input. Hidden wall-clock
reads make historical replay non-deterministic and are forbidden in the pure
projection.

Historical byte-identical replay additionally requires retained exact external
record evidence, the input ledger cutoff, canonicalization manifest, compiler
digest, and policy digest. Where those inputs do not exist, the receipt says
cannot-replay-exactly and identifies the missing custody; it does not fetch a
new record version and call the result historical.

Removal or CID mismatch changes current render eligibility, not the historical
observation. Reverting a subject to an old CID does not revive an old
admission binding or its standing inputs. A valid new exact submission and
admission create a new lifecycle and observation; if the URI+CID content node
already exists, only the new binding can make it current.

The current assembled AppView must apply removal and exact-CID eligibility
before deriving its cluster lens, current counts, representative, ordering, or
qualifying-evidence clock—not merely before final exposure. Only lifecycle
bindings renderable at the requested as-of time may affect current standing.
Historical snapshots remain observer audit artifacts and cannot refresh
current state.

No initial R2-C5 public surface renders historical removed/deleted member
bytes, URIs, or links. A later historical surface requires a separate
disclosure-policy decision covering audience, redaction, deletion, retention,
and receipts. A public cluster explanation cannot become a side door around
removal.

## Corpus custody and disclosure boundary

OQ-2 decides authenticity and trust evidence; it does not decide how long
ROADMAP2 may retain exact payloads or who may inspect derived joins. Before any
non-synthetic R2-C1 corpus or R2-C5 surface is proposed, R2-Q10 must select an
observer-evidence lifecycle and a packet must include a field-disclosure
matrix covering:

- exact record bytes and durable evidence objects;
- raw and normalized URLs, strongRefs, and media CIDs;
- admission-binding, cluster, origin, and relation graphs;
- actor joins, high-waters, internal digests, snapshot IDs, and lineage;
- explanation receipts and forcing-case artifacts;
- evaluation corpora, annotations, and participant responses, if any; and
- contest submitter identity, evidence, status, resolution, and response
  artifacts.

For every class the matrix pins collection purpose, allowed audience,
authentication and authorization, least-privilege roles, access auditing,
export/download controls, incident revocation, public/API/log treatment,
storage and backup locations, retention deadline, redaction or destruction
behavior, and the tombstone or cannot-replay-exactly result left when payload
custody ends. An audience name alone is not an access-control boundary.

Suppression is observer-relative. Once communitywatch has authentically
observed an eligibility-ending event and applied it to the active projection,
new observer responses suppress the affected subject and create no additional
removed-member oracle. The matrix pins publication-cache expiry/purge and
records its limits; previously returned or independently cached results cannot
be clawed back. Public suppression is separate from internal retention.
Append-only observation facts do not imply indefinite raw-payload custody.

“Inspectable” never means publicly enumerable by default. Public explanations
must not reveal secret-bearing URL components, internal evidence locations, or
membership-testable content digests, and must not reveal removed subjects
through count deltas, lineage, or identifier churn.

The per-field matrix is necessary but not sufficient. R2-P4's joint-view
audit evaluates the complete declared observer history: chronological and
clustered lists, detail/explain/contest endpoints, counts and deltas, lineage
and stable IDs, ordering and pagination, cursors and conditional responses,
authenticated/public variants, retained before/after and cross-cutoff
responses, adaptive query sequences, allowed role/account coalitions, caches,
and the optional feed. For each protected fact it keeps **operational
sufficiency** and **within the declared disclosure bound** as independent
verdicts. Views certified against one common explicit disclosure bound compose
within that bound; merely non-determining views, or endpoints approved under
weaker or incomparable budgets, need not. A single composite privacy or
usefulness score is not admissible.

## Initial discussion-standing semantics

The initial state vocabulary is deliberately qualitative:

| State | Minimum meaning | Does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| active | qualifying evidence falls inside the current policy window | true, urgent, popular, or independently confirmed |
| cooling | the active window elapsed but the stale boundary has not | probably false or safe to ignore |
| stale | the declared freshness boundary elapsed | false, disproven, or erased |
| unknown | required time, evidence, or coverage is unavailable | inactive, zero, or false |

These states annotate the alternate lens. They do not hide an otherwise
renderable admitted post from the canonical chronological view.

The initial carrier is a content-addressed observer view-policy artifact scoped
to one community and ratified by the principal selected under R2-Q11. Its
receipt and every rendered state identify the projection producer, ratifier
and role, effective time, version, and superseded/withdrawn policy. The UI
must not imply that an observer-computed policy is community-signed. The
artifact is not an ATProto record, communitywatch reads it only as projection
configuration, and communityd never reads it. A signed community policy record
would be a separate guarantee candidate with its own writer and doctrine
review.

R2-Q4's policy must declare candidate-basis equivalence, applicability, the
selection coordinates and partial order, precedence, and tie/conflict behavior
before current-basis selection is checkable. Only applicable bases participate;
an irrelevant or invalid newer wrapper cannot deny service to an otherwise
qualifying basis. Under the ratified order, selection examines all applicable
bases at the exact cutoff—including adverse and non-testifying states—before
deciding whether the selected basis can testify. It may not filter to
active/testifying evidence first or fall back to an older permissive basis
after a later applicable adverse basis. The initial proposed tie rule is
unknown or a typed refusal; Q4 must ratify it. Revalidation or refresh
constructs a later attributed basis and a new projection generation; it never
mutates, re-times, or re-labels the earlier basis. The internal receipt
accounts for the selected evidence/time slot and superseded bases; public
explanation exposes only the R2-Q10-permitted representation.

“Resolved” and “disputed” are intentionally absent. Resolution needs a
separate answer about who can assert a terminating relation and what
operational consequence that assertion has. Disagreement needs more than two
posts that use incompatible words. Until those questions have forcing cases,
the system can show attributed later observations and exact declared
relationships without issuing either state.

The generic first policy is a research instrument, not a universal decay law.
Typed profiles such as minutes for a local hazard and years for a research
paper come only after retained evidence demonstrates that one policy fails in
at least two predeclared domains.

## Surfaces

### Canonical community AppView

The exact product surface is the community AppView because it can return
custom objects containing URI+CID members, evidence, state, generations, and
refusals. A future ATProto-facing API should use custom XRPC query NSIDs served
at paths of the form /xrpc/{NSID}; ordinary web routes may separately use
human-friendly paths.

Prospective views:

- chronological — the existing exact admitted-post view;
- clustered by one declared hard-anchor basis;
- cluster snapshot detail;
- explanation receipt;
- predecessor, split, and merge lineage.

The first UI does not invent an event headline. It may show a representative
admitted post, literal source metadata with provenance, the structural anchor,
member/origin/amplification counts, standing state, and explanation.

Because initial clusters overlap, the UI must say which basis is being viewed.
A combined one-card-per-event edition would require another declared
projection policy; it cannot emerge accidentally from graph connectivity.
Each view policy also pins multiple-anchor overlap/dedup behavior and orders
clusters reverse chronologically by latest currently eligible admission time,
then by cluster snapshot ID. Discussion standing, relation activity, volume,
and engagement are not ordering inputs.

### Optional stock-client feed

A standard feed skeleton contains post AT URIs, not arbitrary cluster objects
or admitted CIDs. A stock AppView hydrates those mutable URIs as ordinary
posts. Therefore a future compatibility feed can, at most:

- select one deterministic representative URI for each display cluster that
  has at least one currently renderable exact member;
- preflight the current record and suppress a known CID mismatch;
- expose only non-sensitive context through feedContext; and
- point users toward the exact community AppView.

The preflight has an unavoidable hydration race. The feed must describe itself
as lossy and must never promise exact admitted-version parity. If no safe
representative exists, suppression is preferable to substituting a different
record silently.

Representative selection is view policy even without a score. A candidate
policy would use a declared precedence over positively observed structural
relation classes, then first verified observation, then a stable deterministic
tie-break. It must not label absence of a derivation edge as originality.
Discussion-standing state does not filter the feed; stale and unknown clusters
remain eligible when they have a renderable member. Volume, engagement,
regularity, and author status are excluded inputs.

This adapter is not S2's admitted-set parity feed and cannot quietly amend S2.
It waits for an accepted R2-C5 slice-exit receipt and verdict, S2's
process/firewall seam, and OQ-R2.

## Contestability boundary

Explanation is necessary but not sufficient. Before controlled reviewer
exposure under R2-C5, R2-Q12 must select a contest posture and its packet must
pin:

- who may contest an anchor normalization, membership edge, origin relation,
  standing clock/profile, or disclosure;
- the exact generation, artifact, edge, state, and evidence being challenged;
- filing, intake validation/acceptance, merits resolution, reliance effect,
  emergency shutdown, and remediation as distinct transitions;
- contest evidence custody and disclosure;
- pending, upheld, rejected, withdrawn, and unresolved presentation;
- urgent whole-lens shutdown behavior for disclosure or visibility failures;
  and
- the authenticated resolver principal, decision standard, status receipt,
  and remediation recommendation without rewriting challenged history.

A contest is observer feedback, not admission, removal, moderation, event
truth, or community authority. There are no hidden per-cluster overrides.
Filing alone changes no reliance posture. A separately authenticated intake
act may accept a contest for review and, only if the selected posture says so,
annotate or suspend reliance on the experimental lens; it is still not
revocation, merits resolution, or remediation authority.
Manual feedback may not mutate a current graph in place or become runtime
training/annotation input without a separately governed artifact contract.
A contest resolution only nominates remediation. Compiler changes follow the
ordinary governed release path; policy and disclosure changes require the
applicable R2-Q11 and R2-Q10 ratifiers and create a new generation. A
receipted emergency whole-lens shutdown may occur before merits are resolved,
but it neither upholds the contest nor authorizes a replacement rule.

## Current protocol constraints

Verified against official sources on 2026-07-16:

- [Tap](https://docs.bsky.app/blog/introducing-tap) can discover repositories
  containing a configured signal collection, independently filter emitted
  record collections, verify repository data, and backfill current repository
  state. On desynchronization it attempts resynchronization from the
  authoritative PDS; recovery is not unconditional. It is beta and not an
  archival record history: backfill and resync events describe current
  repository state, not prior record versions. Delivery is at least once;
  live events provide per-repository barriers, backfill/resync work may
  proceed concurrently, and there is no global order. Consumers must tolerate
  duplicates and live/backfill interleaving. Subject to the selected OQ-2
  answer, one possible adapter would place Tap inside communitywatch's
  observer trust boundary rather than treating simplified downstream JSON as
  self-authenticating. This file selects no adapter or trust mode. See also the
  [Tap README](https://github.com/bluesky-social/indigo/blob/main/cmd/tap/README.md)
  and [repository sync specification](https://atproto.com/specs/sync).
- [Jetstream](https://docs.bsky.app/blog/jetstream) deliberately omits the
  signed repository/MST material needed to verify “who said what.” It may be a
  prototype transport, but it cannot satisfy exact authenticated custody by
  itself.
- [ATProto labels](https://atproto.com/specs/label) attach a short value from
  one source to one subject URI (a record AT URI or account DID), with optional
  CID, expiry, and negation. Encoding a many-member, community-relative graph
  through labels would require fanout and external interpretation. More
  importantly, this repository's doctrine restricts labels to presentation
  hints.
- The
  [getFeedSkeleton Lexicon](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/getFeedSkeleton.json)
  requires a post URI and offers no admitted-CID field or arbitrary cluster
  object. [AT URIs are non-content-addressed references to record paths](https://atproto.com/specs/at-uri-scheme);
  their contents may change or disappear.
  [feedContext](https://github.com/bluesky-social/atproto/blob/main/lexicons/app/bsky/feed/defs.json)
  is bounded client-visible pass-through context that may return with
  interaction telemetry, not a secret or an authority channel.
- [Custom schemas](https://docs.bsky.app/docs/advanced-guides/custom-schemas)
  and [XRPC](https://atproto.com/specs/xrpc) permit richer application-specific
  query outputs under custom Lexicons. A stock Bluesky client will not render
  those custom cluster objects; a community web/client surface must.

The first experiment does not need a new live ingest path. It should use
retained V0 evidence or frozen fixtures. A production Tap integration is a
later observer-only task, inherits the OQ-2 evidence-boundary decision, and
must make gaps and current-state-only backfill explicit.

## Evaluation contract

“The clustered view feels better” is not sufficient evidence. Before any
R2-C5 ratification packet, the study must preregister:

- corpus and exclusion rules;
- source population, as-of/window, sampling unit, lifecycle eligibility,
  strata, deterministic selector/seed, exclusions, attrition, and natural
  singleton/unknown/base rates;
- development and untouched evaluation splits separated by anchor
  series/lineage and time;
- exact hard-anchor algorithms and versions;
- expected cluster ambiguities and bridge cases;
- structural, policy-conformance, and human-judgment oracle boundaries;
- annotation unit, visible context, rubric/version, blinding, abstention,
  disagreement, and adjudication rules;
- comparison views;
- coherence questions;
- false-merge and false-split review method;
- stale-resurrection cases;
- whether reviewers can distinguish one origin from many amplifications;
- whether explanations let reviewers contest grouping and time state;
- accessibility and cognitive-load checks;
- privacy and recomposition risks;
- staged shadow/reviewer/public exposure and early-stop rules;
- evaluation-manifest, raw-response, and analysis custody;
- success, failure, and inconclusive thresholds; and
- the migration/packet-preparation conclusion each outcome supports.

At minimum, failure means one or more of:

- reviewers systematically read a cluster as a truth or event verdict;
- unsafe transitive merges exceed the predeclared budget;
- origin accounting is mistaken for independent corroboration;
- stale material becomes more alarming because of the cluster presentation;
- explanation cannot identify the exact evidence for membership or state;
- missing coverage is rendered as zero;
- the alternate lens silently changes admission visibility;
- exact historical replay depends on fetching mutable current records; or
- the projection creates privacy or harassment leverage disproportionate to
  its coherence benefit.

An inconclusive result does not satisfy the evidence predicate for preparing a
migration/ratification packet for soft semantics, typed decay, or a stock
feed. No evaluation result authorizes implementation.

### Evaluation oracle, sampling, and exposure gates

“Gold” has three disjoint meanings and must never collapse:

1. structural facts reproduced from retained exact bytes;
2. discussion-standing conformance reproduced from a pinned policy and clocks;
   and
3. human judgments about usefulness, ambiguity, explanation, and
   comprehension.

Human annotation is not ground truth for event identity, truth,
corroboration, independence, correction, supersession, or resolution.
Independent judgments and individual annotations remain retained; the study
pins an agreement statistic with uncertainty, an adjudication rule, and an
unresolved-disagreement outcome. Majority vote cannot manufacture a fact, and
ambiguous strata cannot disappear into an average. Annotation used later at
runtime would be a new governed input, not evaluation evidence.

Sampling must preserve natural singletons, unknowns, and negative/base rates
rather than selecting only useful-looking clusters. Negative controls include
generic or high-fanout URLs, one URL reused across unrelated episodes,
live/mutating pages, exact media reused in new context, several same-kind
anchors in one post, related-looking discussion with no admissible hard
anchor, missing/conflicting times, removals, and CID mismatches. Development
and untouched evaluation sets are split by anchor lineage and time so related
records cannot leak across them.

Primary endpoints and units of analysis are preregistered. At minimum they
separate unsafe merge/split incidence by anchor kind and severity; refusal,
unknown, singleton, and coverage rates; origin/amplification interpretation;
standing/explanation comprehension errors; task error/time/cognitive load
against a content-parity chronological baseline; and privacy,
removed-content, exact-version, and visibility violations. Results are
reported per stratum with uncertainty. Aggregate coherence benefit cannot
compensate for a privacy, exact-version, canonical-visibility, or
verdict/urgency-confusion guardrail failure.

Intended users—not only expert fixture reviewers—must be tested on the exact
anti-collapse claims: cluster is not event/truth; active is not true/urgent;
stale is not false/resolved; unknown is not none; and origin count is not
independent confirmation. If participants beyond the repository's ordinary
review team are involved, the packet also pins consent/withdrawal,
sensitive-content handling, researcher access, recruitment/compensation,
response retention/deletion, and incident handling.

For every human-facing endpoint, preregistration also pins the intended-user
sampling frame, participant inclusion/exclusion criteria, recruitment and
attrition accounting, a sample-size or minimum-precision rationale, and
multiplicity handling across primary endpoints. A feasibility cohort cannot
support claims about an unrepresented population.

Exposure proceeds only:

~~~text
frozen offline fixtures
  -> silent shadow computation with no product/authority consequence
  -> controlled reviewer preview
  -> public opt-in after an accepted exposure gate
~~~

Every forward transition names an advance principal and authentication method,
an exact evidence predicate, and a content-addressed transition receipt.
Enforcement fails closed if the predicate or receipt is absent. A halt
principal may stop or roll back exposure but no reviewer result, elapsed time,
or operator role coincidence advances a stage by inference.

The study preregisters early-stop triggers, maximum looks/runs, the principal
who can halt exposure, and retained stop evidence. Privacy leakage,
removed-content exposure, canonical-visibility change, exact-version mismatch,
or guardrail-level verdict/urgency confusion stops exposure immediately. Any
algorithm, corpus, rubric, policy, or UI change after seeing results creates a
new version, preregistration, and untouched holdout; failed runs stay in the
record.

A content-addressed evaluation receipt pins corpus/split digests, sampling and
randomization seeds, as-of time, UI/algorithm/policy builds, annotation
rubric/tool version, participant assignment/order, exclusions, raw-response
custody, analysis code/environment, thresholds, deviations, and every result.
It makes judgment auditable with uncertainty; it does not promise
byte-identical human answers.

standing/representative manipulation. Admission bounds scope; it does not make
input benign. Unrecognized membership or refresh cases refuse.

Each claimed invariant declares its admitted input basis, excluded
perturbations, and operating regime. Fixtures pair a positive inhabitant with
the boundary case and name the tempting evaluator rather than merely omitting
it: current URI for exact CID; cross-kind transitive union; volume/DID count as
independence; admission, discussion, custody, computation, or packet-generation
time as evidence freshness; partial as complete; older generation after
eligibility advanced; or filed contest as override. An always-refuse
implementation cannot satisfy the suite vacuously.

Every promoted candidate pins evidence bytes; anchors and memberships per
member; cluster size; lineage depth; generation rows/bytes/runtime; storage
growth; API pagination/response/concurrency; and any context/model work.
Exceeding a bound produces a typed resource/coverage refusal. Silent
truncation, hidden sampling, order-dependent output, or publication of a
partial generation as complete is forbidden.

Budgets are scoped to the named candidate, run, and unit and are allocated
before use. A later approval cannot retroactively legitimize an overrun, and
unused budget in another candidate/run is not implicitly transferable.

Hostile or oversized input fails at the narrowest deterministic member,
cluster, generation, or request unit that preserves honest coverage. R2
workers, queues, queries, and storage have budgets isolated from canonical
ingest and the chronological surface, so exhausting the experimental lens
cannot starve the fallback it promises.

The serving matrix covers source lag, detected gaps, current-state-only resync,
resolver failure, incomplete rebuild, failed generation, missing policy/model,
and resource exhaustion for each surface. It may select the last complete
generation with disclosed high-water/staleness only when that generation's
lifecycle-eligibility cutoff/digest matches the current canonical projection.
If admission, removal, or CID eligibility has advanced, the clustered surface
refuses and may fall back to the unchanged chronological view. Coverage repair
cannot masquerade as semantic merge/split; a partial smaller cluster cannot
look healthy, and a stale generation cannot expose an ineligible member.

Every negative result states its scope: one member/edge/cluster/attempt, the
named corpus/window/catalog at an exact cutoff, or—only with an exact
completeness witness—a global conclusion. Missing relations or singleton
output under partial coverage cannot mean that no relation exists elsewhere.
Anchor or endpoint coverage cannot substitute for exact candidate custody.

### R2-P4 — Executable semantics and refinement assurance

[The formal-assurance seam](../ROADMAP2-FORMAL-ASSURANCE.md) defines the
packet grammar and initial R2-F obligation handles. Its first responsibility
is the conservative boundary between the then-ratified canonical eligibility
generation (with V0 as its baseline) and every R2 consumer; R2 does not gain a
second lifecycle reducer. Exact community-AppView results and the URI-only C6
adapter carry distinct conformance claims.

The packet pins the canonical eligibility rule/generation and snapshot
schema/digests, exact cutoff and as-of, required nonempty stage inventory,
typed stage topology/order and input/output contracts, admitted input basis,
excluded perturbations, operating regime, assumptions/trusted computing base,
finite bounds, success certificates, typed and scoped refusals, stable
location IDs, and canonical serialization/domain separation. Any prior-art
mapping pins repository/commit, module/symbol, source custody/status,
adopted-statement-versus-analogy, and source nonclaims. Every constitutional
law and candidate-local refusal carried by R2-P1 is marked applicable or
inapplicable with rationale; each applicable item is classified as
type-enforced, proved,
bounded-model-checked, property-tested, differential-tested,
static-boundary-checked, human/governance-only, or unmodeled.

Machine obligations include positive inhabitants, named tempting evaluators,
premise-ablation or boundary countermodels, and minimized regression fixtures.
The correspondence map binds every abstract coordinate to exact schema fields,
implementation functions, configuration/toolchain digests, enforcement points,
and executable tests. A runtime path receipt proves that the published output
traversed the declared connected stage topology at the same unit and cutoff; a
disconnected or bypassed green stage does not count. A conformance receipt pins
the commands and artifacts it actually checked. An empty stage list,
all-refuse implementation, green toy model without a runtime mapping, or
passing runtime without the declared law cannot close the packet. An
applicable item closes only through a passed required machine gate or its
separately named human/governance gate; otherwise it blocks slice exit.
Applicable unmodeled or unresolved items cannot be waived nonblocking unless
the underlying law/refusal first changes through R2-P1's promoted-question,
exact-change-set, authenticated-ruling path and the revised inventory makes
the old item inapplicable or superseded.

Formalization may precede a forcing case, but neither its existence nor a
green result migrates a candidate. No method is prescribed: Lean is optional,
and no proof, model, or test confers authority or establishes unmodeled social,
privacy, protocol, or policy claims.

## Decision-seed rules

Each entry below is an **analysis seed that must be rebuilt against retained
forcing evidence**. It borrows the operator-question shape without pretending
that a question has been promoted or that the provisional answer classes are
packet-ready.

- **Why now** records why the choice is foreseeable, not why the operator must
  answer today.
- **Promotion evidence** is the forcing-case bundle required before asking.
  “Not yet observed” is a stop, not an invitation to invent examples.
- **Admissible answers** are bounded choices that can coexist with current
  doctrine. Constitutionally invalid shortcuts are listed separately as
  excluded non-answers.
- **Recommended answer** is an agent-prepared recommendation, never a ruling.
- **Consequences** name authority, guarantees, tasks, document amendments, and
  replay/fork effects.
- Every consequence describes only how the answer would satisfy or leave open
  this seed's blocker. No task becomes eligible without migration to the
  canonical roadmap, BUILD ratification, all independent decisions and gates,
  and a valid controller-entry packet.
- The answer classes are sketches. Promotion must replace them with mutually
  exclusive, complete joint options that pin numerical, schema, artifact,
  collection, fanout, retention, process, and policy bounds. Overlap or a
  missing bound requires splitting or rewriting the seed.
- **Novelty condition** is binding: evidence that fits no answer requires a
  replacement packet. No nearest-answer inference or hybrid selection.
- This document carries no selection, signature, principal, timestamp, or
  mutable ruling field. If a question is promoted, any ruling exists only in a
  separately authenticated operator-decision receipt.

### R2-Q1 — Corpus and custody boundary

**Question:** Which records may the first correlation compiler inspect and
which may become members?

**Why now:** Every later explanation, privacy boundary, and replay guarantee
depends on a finite corpus and exact-version evidence.

**Promotion evidence:** Not yet observed. Require the authenticated V0-M8
“feels like a real place” receipt, a valid post-V0 lane, a finite retained
corpus with repeated hard anchors, an OQ-2 ruling, and exact
evidence-custody/coverage examples. Demonstrated missing admitted-only context
is an answer-B fit condition, not a prerequisite for promoting A or C.

**Admissible answers:**

- **A — Exact admitted versions only.** Only exact URI+CID subjects with
  retained admissible evidence may be inspected or clustered.
- **B — Exact admitted members plus bounded one-hop context.** Membership
  remains exact and admitted, but explicitly referenced records may be fetched
  as non-member context with independent coverage and privacy receipts. A
  promoted option must pin allowed collections, exactly one traversal hop,
  per-member/per-generation reference and byte caps, rate limits, retention,
  and failure behavior.
- **C — Decline correlation.** Keep the chronological admission view and do
  not build the anchor ledger.

**Recommended answer:** A for R2-C1 through R2-C5. Consider B only after named
missing-context cases survive review.

**Evidence supporting it:** Admission supplies the scope boundary; exact CID
preserves the existing guarantee; the problem remains small and replayable.

**Evidence against it:** An admitted quotation may omit the original context,
and old exact records may no longer be obtainable unless already retained.

**Residual uncertainty:** OQ-2 record-retention and verification boundary;
coverage when referenced records are deleted or changed.

| C | no change | would leave every R2 correlation candidate unsupported | existing V0 replay only |

**Excluded non-answers:** Treat the current record at an admitted URI as the
admitted CID; crawl the full network and infer community relevance; silently
turn referenced context into cluster membership.

**Novelty condition:** A case requiring more than bounded one-hop context, or
one whose exact evidence cannot be retained safely, fits no answer. Escalate
with a replacement packet; do not widen B by interpretation.

### R2-Q3 — Origin and amplification semantics

| B | none | would satisfy this seed with a narrower literal-equality ledger | replay pins syntax validator/version; false splits remain explicit |
| C | none | would satisfy only the protocol-native subset and leave URL/media clustering unsupported | replay pins exact native strongRefs only |

**Excluded non-answers:** Fetch a URL to decide identity; strip an unlisted
parameter; use title/headline similarity; perceptual media matching; fuzzy
text, embedding, entity, or location inference; add a new hard kind without a
replacement decision.

**Novelty condition:** A proposed anchor or normalization transform that fits
none of the complete promoted tables requires a replacement packet. It remains
unavailable rather than inheriting the nearest rule.

### R2-Q10 — Observer evidence lifecycle and disclosure

**Question:** How long may communitywatch retain exact and derived evidence,
and who may inspect each class after removal, source deletion/CID change, or
corpus exit?

**Why now:** Exact replay benefits from retained bytes while removal, privacy,
and data minimization constrain continued custody. OQ-2 authenticates evidence
but does not settle its lifecycle.

**Promotion evidence:** Not yet observed. Require the complete field inventory,
representative removal/deletion/CID-change cases, proposed audiences, storage
and backup topology, replay needs, the R2-P2 threat review, and R2-P4's
joint-view disclosure audit over endpoints, deltas, identifiers, cursors, and
caches.

**Admissible answers:**

- **A — Fixed nonrenewable retention by evidence class.** After an
  eligibility-ending event is observed and applied, suppress the subject from
  new public observer responses; retain each R2 evidence class to a pinned
  nonrenewable deadline; then destroy it and preserve only the minimum
  non-sensitive tombstone/manifest with cannot-replay-exactly.
- **B — Purpose-bound renewable audit custody.** Keep specified exact classes
  under named roles to a finite expiry. Each continued audit period requires
  a separately authenticated purpose review and a new finite expiry; there is
  no implicit renewal. Public suppression still follows active eligibility,
  and final purge leaves a minimum tombstone and replay refusal.
- **C — No non-synthetic longitudinal R2 payload custody.** Process live exact
  evidence only within a bounded computation and retain no R2 exact payload,
  anchor, relationship, contest, or evaluation corpus afterward. Exact replay
  uses only synthetic or separately approved frozen fixtures with their own
  receipts; no longitudinal or public clustered research is supported.

**Recommended answer:** A. It makes replay loss explicit instead of treating
append-only facts as permission for indefinite payload custody.

**Evidence supporting it:** Class-specific expiry limits recomposition and
disclosure risk while preserving enough manifest evidence to explain why
exact replay ended.

**Evidence against it:** Nonrenewable expiry prevents later byte-exact replay,
recalibration, and investigation of long-horizon cluster behavior. B preserves
more audit value at the cost of repeated custody decisions.

**Residual uncertainty:** Exact deadlines, backup/purge verification,
exceptional holds, URL-component redaction, participant/contest handling, and
which tombstone fields remain membership-testable. An exceptional hold under A
is novelty, not an inferred renewal.

| Answer | Authority/guarantee consequence | Tasks and documents | Replay/fork consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | none; observer custody only | would require class inventory, fixed TTL, timed purge/readback, joint-view disclosure, backup, and tombstone gates | exact replay ends per class at the nonrenewable expiry and receipts say so |
| B | none, but largest observer/privacy surface | would require named-role access, authenticated purpose reviews, finite renewals, joint-view disclosure, purge, and non-enumerability gates | exact replay continues only through receipted custody periods; final purge is explicit |
| C | none | would permit bounded live computation and frozen/offline fixtures only, with the same disclosure audit for any exposed result; longitudinal/public R2 work remains unsupported | replay is limited to the approved frozen corpus; live computations are not exactly replayable |

**Excluded non-answers:** Append-only means indefinite raw retention; a digest
is automatically safe to expose; public accessibility continues after
removal; backup copies are outside the lifecycle; silent destruction followed
by a claim of exact replay.

**Novelty condition:** An evidence class, audience, or hold purpose absent from
the selected matrix requires a replacement packet. It cannot inherit the
nearest retention or disclosure class.

### R2-Q11 — Projection-policy principal and attribution

**Question:** Whose human decision ratifies a community-scoped observer
view-policy artifact, and how is that decision attributed?

**Why now:** The governed-loop operator is repository governance, not
automatically the community administrator. Calling an artifact
“community-local” must not make observer configuration appear community-signed.

**Promotion evidence:** Not yet observed. Require a proposed R2-C4 policy,
available principal/authentication roles, effective/supersession/withdrawal
examples, receipt payload, and UI/API attribution mockups.

**Admissible answers:**

- **A — Repository-governance observer policy.** The authenticated repository
  governance operator ratifies it for the experiment; every receipt and UI
  names the projection producer and labels the state as observer-computed, not
  community-signed.
- **B — Separately authenticated community-administrator approval.** A defined
  community administrator independently authenticates the non-ATProto policy
  artifact under a new receipt contract; UI distinguishes
  administrator-approved observer policy from a signed community record;
  communitywatch still does not write and communityd does not read it.
- **C — No qualitative policy state.** Publish attributed raw ages/clocks only
  under R2-Q4-B and avoid a policy-ratifier claim.

**Recommended answer:** A for the first research trial. It uses the actual
governance principal without inventing portable community authority.

**Evidence supporting it:** A has an existing review principal and cleanly
attributes the experimental projection. It preserves the observer/emitter
firewall.

**Evidence against it:** A is operator policy about a community rather than a
decision by that community's administrator; users may reasonably prefer B.

**Residual uncertainty:** Whether the same person holds several roles, the
authentication contract B would require, policy withdrawal effects, and UI
language that remains intelligible without laundering authority.

| Answer | Authority/guarantee consequence | Tasks and documents | Replay/fork consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | none; repository-governance testimony only | would satisfy the initial observer-policy ratifier/attribution blocker | pin operator receipt, producer, effective interval, and UI attribution; forks reratify |
| B | no ATProto authority, but adds a new authenticated principal/receipt seam | would require a separate identity, authentication, receipt, conflict, and attribution contract | preserve approval as testimony; forks cannot import it as authority |
| C | none | would leave qualitative R2-C4/R2-C7 state unsupported | replay attributed clocks only |

**Excluded non-answers:** Hidden or unauthenticated ratifier; UI says “the
community says active” for observer configuration; communitywatch signs a
policy record; communityd reads observer policy; a signed community policy
record enters this testimony slice.

**Novelty condition:** A principal or portable-authority requirement outside
these classes requires a replacement packet or a separate guarantee
candidate. Role coincidence cannot be inferred as role equivalence.

### R2-Q12 — Contest and correction posture

**Question:** How may a person challenge a derived cluster, standing state, or
disclosure without turning feedback into hidden authority?

**Why now:** Explanation makes a result inspectable, but R2-C5 has no current
artifact for lodging, retaining, resolving, or displaying a challenge.

**Promotion evidence:** Not yet observed. Require a controlled-reviewer
preview, challenge taxonomy, representative privacy/removal and false-merge
cases, proposed submitter/audience roles, evidence custody, and response
capacity.

**Admissible answers:**

- **A — Public-stage receipted typed contest channel.** During public opt-in
  exposure, accept exact-artifact challenges from affected subject authors and
  public users under explicit identity/authorization classes. An
  authenticated intake actor applies a pinned acceptance standard and reliance
  rule; an authenticated resolver separately applies the merits standard and
  records evidence, status, and a remediation recommendation. Filing changes
  nothing by itself. Intake may annotate or suspend the experimental lens only
  under the selected rule. Any compiler, policy, disclosure, or refusal change
  uses its separately governed release or R2-Q11/R2-Q10 ratification path.
- **B — Controlled-reviewer contests only.** Use the same immutable
  filing/intake/recommendation separation inside a bounded, authenticated
  review cohort; accept no public contests and do not expose the clustered
  lens publicly.
- **C — Explanation without contest intake.** Restrict R2-C5 to frozen offline
  evaluation artifacts, accept no contests, and run neither interactive
  reviewer nor public exposure.

**Recommended answer:** A before any public opt-in exposure; B is sufficient
only for the controlled-reviewer stage.

**Evidence supporting it:** A gives errors and privacy harms a visible path
without altering admission or letting manual edits become algorithmic truth.

**Evidence against it:** A creates abuse, identity, response-time, evidence
retention, and harassment surfaces; a weakly staffed channel can imply remedy
it cannot deliver.

**Residual uncertainty:** Exact submitter authentication and viewing rules,
subject-author priority or opt-out posture, urgent disclosure response,
response targets, intake and resolver capacity/conflicts, abuse controls, and
whether accepted or unresolved contests annotate or suspend the current lens.

| Answer | Authority/guarantee consequence | Tasks and documents | Replay/fork consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | none; filings, intake acts, and resolutions remain observer testimony | would satisfy the public contestability blocker only with identity, intake/resolver principals, standards, reliance rule, abuse, custody, status, shutdown, and separate remediation-ratification gates | retain challenged generations, filing/intake/resolution receipts, and any separately governed successor; forks may omit testimony |
| B | none | would satisfy controlled review only and leave public exposure unsupported | retain cohort-scoped contests and any separately governed successor artifacts |
| C | none | would leave interactive R2-C5 exposure unsupported | frozen evaluation replay only |

**Excluded non-answers:** Hidden per-cluster override; contest equals removal or
truth; majority complaints determine membership; manual feedback becomes
runtime training data silently; communitywatch emits a correction record;
urgent privacy complaints wait for semantic adjudication before the
experimental lens can be disabled.

**Novelty condition:** A requested author opt-out, appeal authority, portable
contest record, or remedy not represented here requires a replacement packet.
Do not infer it from submitter identity or a resolver recommendation.

## Promotion checklist

Before migrating any R2-C candidate into the canonical roadmap:

1. Retain the exact forcing-case evidence and its limitations.
2. Demonstrate that the current chronological view and existing slice ladder
   cannot answer the question without the proposed semantic expansion.
3. Select one smallest candidate; do not ratify R2-C1 through R2-C5 as a
   convenience bundle.
4. Resolve the applicable R2-P2/R2-P3 shapes and the initial R2-P4
   obligation/refinement ledger, then draft the candidate in canonical roadmap
   field grammar, including proposed permanent S-number, type, authority
   delta, dependencies, replay seam, deliverables, exact machine gates,
   evidence log, and literal Authorized: no.
5. Resolve R2-P1: prepare an exact incubator-to-roadmap migration proposal with
   source/target digests and constraint trace, then have it authenticated and
   applied through the governance mechanism defined for that transition.
6. Promote only decision seeds that genuinely block that migrated slice.
   Replace every “not yet observed” field with content-addressed evidence.
7. Prepare canonical operator-question packets with admissible choices,
   recommendation, counterevidence, residual uncertainty, consequences,
   novelty rule, and exact change sets.
8. Obtain separately authenticated operator-decision receipts. A recommended
   answer is not a receipt.
9. Prepare exact BUILD.md, ROADMAP.md, implementation-plan, test-plan, schema,
   doctrine, and R2-P4 planned-correspondence patches required by the selected
   answers. If an outstanding packet pins changed inputs, replace it under the
   governance contract.
10. Ratify the slice through a human-applied BUILD.md amendment and valid
    slice-ratification receipt.
11. Execute only through a separately valid controller-entry packet.
12. At exit, execute the R2-P4 implementation correspondence and joint-view
    gates, then compare results to the pre-registered success, failure, and
    inconclusive thresholds. Failure or ambiguity leaves the next candidate's
    packet-preparation evidence predicate unsatisfied.

## Non-goals

This document does not authorize or promise:

- event detection across Bluesky or the Atmosphere;
- a canonical claim ontology;
- truth, falsity, credibility, safety, or confidence judgments;
- fact-check labels;
- semantic search or global discovery;
- cross-community surveillance or identity association;
- inferred location or phenomenon type;
- engagement ranking or a trending product;
- automatic moderation, removal, admission, role, membership, or bans;
- changes to user posts, repositories, or PDS behavior;
- a writer path in communitywatch;
- cluster or standing inputs in communityd;
- historical recovery that the source repositories do not provide;
- exact CID hydration in a stock custom feed;
- proof of the whole platform, a social claim, or deployed conformance from an
  abstract artifact alone;
- a mandatory Lean/Mathlib dependency or proof-CI lane;
- semantic or fingerprint reuse from Driftwatch;
- source-code reuse from any sibling without provenance and license review; or
- a second controller lane.

The test is narrower and more interesting: can a bounded community preserve
the provenance and temporal context that a post stream routinely destroys,
while continuing to say exactly what it does not know?

# Roadmap 2 formal-assurance seam

**Status:** non-authorizing preparation scaffold for
[ROADMAP2.md](research/ROADMAP2.md). This document does not amend
[BUILD.md](../BUILD.md), promote a roadmap candidate, select an operator
answer, authorize a Lean artifact, or add a runtime dependency.

## Purpose

ROADMAP2 already states strong anti-collapse laws. The missing seam is an
artifact that connects each applicable prose law to:

1. a bounded, checkable statement;
2. an executable or mechanized witness where that is useful;
3. the exact implementation fields and functions intended to realize it; and
4. runtime evidence that the implementation still corresponds to the
   statement.

Those are separate claims. A theorem beside a divergent reducer is not
conformance, and a passing reducer is not ratification.

The intended scale is small. Formal work may show that a projection cannot
write authority, duplicate delivery cannot inflate an effective origin count,
or a stale current basis cannot be hidden behind an older permissive one. It
does not prove a community, event, source, policy, or social system correct.

## Authority and development boundary

A coherent formal claim may be stated before a runtime forcing case exists.
That lets the contract lead implementation when its scope, hypotheses, and
countermodels are already precise. It changes none of ROADMAP2's promotion
rules:

~~~text
claim stated
  != abstract artifact mechanically checked
  != contract adopted by a migrated candidate
  != implementation mapped to the contract
  != runtime correspondence tested
  != slice accepted
  != work authorized
~~~

No transition in that list is inferred from another. Agent agreement, human
review, a green proof, a model-check result, a passing test, and a release
artifact are review evidence. Only the separately authenticated governance act
recognized for the exact transition can change roadmap or build standing.

A proof, model check, executable specification, type boundary, or property
test is scoped observer testimony. It can establish only its named property
under pinned abstractions, assumptions, bounds, and toolchain. It cannot:

- authenticate ATProto evidence or establish network completeness;
- choose an admissible anchor, time, retention, disclosure, or contest policy;
- establish event identity, truth, independence, corroboration, safety, or
  resolution;
- satisfy privacy, abuse, usability, or human-comprehension review;
- prove that an unmodeled deployment conforms;
- promote an R2-C candidate or select an R2-Q answer; or
- authorize implementation, exposure, publication, or authority records.

Lean is optional. The smallest adequate method wins: a structural type or
package boundary, deterministic reference evaluator, exhaustive finite check,
property/metamorphic test, differential test, static dependency check, or
mechanized theorem. This repository gains no Lean or Mathlib dependency merely
because the prior art is useful.

## Canonical eligibility seam

R2 does not independently reimplement admission, removal, CID terminality, or
chronological-view authority semantics. It consumes a canonical, read-only
eligibility generation produced by the rules ratified for the canonical
chronological projection at that time. [The V0 state machine](V0-STATE-MACHINE.md)
is the baseline, not a forever-frozen reducer. The candidate packet pins the
rule digest, materialized generation, snapshot schema, and canonical
serialization, including at least:

- community DID, projection version/digest, as-of time, and input high-water;
- exact subject URI and CID plus lifecycle/binding identity;
- projected V0 lifecycle state and current render eligibility;
- canonical admission time/order coordinates; and
- coverage, pending inputs, and typed refusal facts.

At a common as-of, cutoff, and eligibility generation:

~~~text
current exact-capable R2 community-AppView versions
  ⊆ then-canonical renderable exact versions
~~~

This is an **eligibility-conservative/no-visibility-expansion** law. It is set
inclusion, not the observational-distinguishability relation sometimes named
`Refines` in view-semantics work; a grouping or omitting lens may be
observationally coarser while still satisfying this law.

Erasing R2-only fields and disabling the alternate lens leaves the canonical
chronological projection byte/content-equivalent under its declared
serialization. R2 may omit or group eligible items in an explicitly lossy
alternate surface. It may never add an ineligible item or change canonical
validity, terminality, removal, or chronological order. Historical audit
snapshots are a separate type and can retain non-current observations under
R2-Q10; they can never enter the current renderable projection by coercion or
fallback. Historical evaluation exists only for retained materialized
generations and their pinned rules unless a separate slice authorizes another
canonical replay contract; R2 cannot invent an arbitrary historical-as-of
reducer.

R2-C6 is deliberately weaker. Its feed skeleton returns a URI only. It must
show that the URI was selected from an eligible exact URI+CID in its pinned
canonical generation and suppress a mismatch known before response, but a
stock AppView may hydrate a later record version. That downstream hydration
and race are disclosed outside exact-subset conformance; neither the feed nor
its UI may claim admitted-CID rendering.

## R2-P4 packet shape

R2-P4 is a cross-cutting preparation packet, not a candidate or operator
question. Its initial form accompanies R2-C1 migration and it grows only with
the semantics a later candidate actually introduces.

Closure is phased. Before BUILD ratification, the packet carries the bounded
claims, abstract checks, counterexamples, planned schemas/symbols, and exact
future conformance gates—never an unauthorized implementation. After a slice
is separately authorized and implemented, its exit packet adds the actual
symbol/digest mapping, executed conformance results, and runtime evidence. A
pre-build plan cannot impersonate the later correspondence receipt.

Each obligation card records:

```text
obligation_id
candidate_and_unit
bounded_claim
forbidden_effect
explicit_nonclaims

admitted_input_basis
excluded_perturbations
operating_regime
assumptions_and_trusted_computing_base
finite_or_resource_bounds

positive_witness
boundary_fixture
named_tempting_evaluator
premise_ablation_or_countermodel

success_certificate
typed_refusals
refusal_scope
required_stage_ids
stage_topology_and_order
stage_input_output_contracts
stable_location_keys

assurance_mode_and_status
model_or_checker_digest
toolchain_and_dependency_digests
abstract_to_schema_function_config_test_map
runtime_path_correspondence
conformance_command_and_result_receipt

prior_art_repository_and_commit
prior_art_module_and_symbol
prior_art_custody_or_status
prior_art_relation: adopted_statement | analogy_only | none
prior_art_explicit_nonclaims

supersedes
residual_open_findings
authority_effect: none
```

`refusal_scope` is mandatory. It distinguishes at least:

- this member, edge, cluster, stage, attempt, or request failed;
- no result exists in this named corpus/window/catalog at this exact cutoff;
  and
- a global nonexistence claim, which is unavailable without an explicit exact
  completeness witness.

“No relation found here” must never render as “no relation exists.” Anchor or
endpoint coverage is not exact candidate custody.

Assurance state is recorded on independent axes rather than one green badge:

| Axis | Example values |
|---|---|
| statement custody | proposed, mechanically checked, adopted contract, superseded |
| implementation correspondence | absent, mapped, differential-tested, refinement-proved |
| runtime evidence | absent, fixture, shadow, controlled exposure, public observation |
| authority | none; governed elsewhere |

Every constitutional law and candidate-local refusal carried by R2-P1 is
marked applicable or inapplicable to the exact candidate, with a reviewable
rationale. Each applicable item is then classified as type-enforced, proved,
bounded-model-checked, property-tested, differential-tested,
static-boundary-checked, human/governance-only, or unmodeled. `Unmodeled` is an
open finding, not silent success. Before slice exit, every applicable item has
exactly one disposition: its required machine gate passed; its separately
named human/governance gate passed; or blocking. An applicable unmodeled or
unresolved item is blocking. No reviewer may mark it optional or waive it
nonblocking unless the underlying law/refusal first changes through R2-P1's
promoted-question, exact-change-set, authenticated-ruling path and the new
inventory review makes the old item inapplicable or superseded. Hash collision
resistance, clock behavior, repository authenticity, external completeness,
and similar environmental premises remain disclosed assumptions unless
separately established; calling something an assumption does not waive an
applicable obligation whose claim depends on it.

## Pipeline and recomposition laws

A composed R2 artifact declares a nonempty inventory of required stage IDs,
typed input/output edges, topology and order, and the stage that owns the
published output. Missing, disconnected, bypassed, or unevaluated required
stages are typed refusals; vacuous `all []` success is forbidden. For the
claimed unit and cutoff:

1. the composed result satisfies its declared contract only if every required
   stage satisfies its own local contract and a runtime path receipt proves
   that the published output traversed the declared connected stage path;
2. each constitutional law and refusal is preserved or blocks recomposition;
   a local nonconstitutional guarantee may weaken only through the separately
   governed change mechanism applicable to its adopted contract, never merely
   by naming a replacement contract;
3. a later success cannot erase an earlier refusal or retroactively authorize
   an earlier action;
4. repair performs a new evaluation and, for persisted compiler output, a new
   generation while retaining the failed receipt;
5. adapters preserve refusal identity and scope;
6. an exact explanation names stage, domain, unit, and cause;
7. location decoration cannot change the base result, and an exact pinpoint
   claim requires unique stable location IDs; and
8. failure remains at the narrowest honest unit and cannot starve or corrupt
   the canonical chronological fallback.

Many-to-one refusal mapping is allowed only when the surface explicitly says
it is a lossy summary and links to the preserved exact causes. A surface that
claims exact explanation may not collapse distinct causes.

## Joint-view disclosure audit

A per-field or per-endpoint disclosure matrix is necessary but insufficient.
The audit first declares the observer model: what response history may be
retained, which adaptive query sequences and as-of/cutoff choices are allowed,
and which public/authenticated roles or accounts may pool observations. It
then enumerates the authorized closure of that observer history across:

- chronological and clustered lists;
- cluster detail and explanation endpoints;
- contest intake, status, and resolution views;
- counts, deltas, lineage, stable identifiers, and refusal changes;
- pagination, cursors, ordering, timing, and conditional responses;
- public and authenticated variants and permitted role/account coalitions;
- retained before/after responses and cross-generation/cross-cutoff queries;
- adaptive query sequences and timing observations;
- publication caches and cache invalidation behavior; and
- the optional stock-feed adapter.

For each protected quantity—removed membership, secret URL components, actor
joins, internal evidence locations, contest identity, or another selected
class—the audit declares which distinctions a permitted observer may learn.
It evaluates the whole joined view, not only each component. At least one
concrete forbidden pair demonstrates the failure when a join exceeds the
budget.

Two verdicts remain separate:

```text
operationally sufficient for the declared explanation/review task
within the declared disclosure bound for the selected audience
```

Both must pass. One composite score or `safe: true` bit is inadmissible. Views
certified against the same explicit disclosure bound compose within that
bound. Individual nondetermination, or separate approval under weaker or
incomparable budgets, does not imply that a longitudinal or adaptive join with
other endpoints, deltas, IDs, roles, or caches stays within the selected bound.
Any common-bound certificate is evaluated on the declared history, query, and
coalition carrier above, not on an isolated snapshot silently substituted for
it.

## Minimum counterexample suite

Every negative law also has a positive inhabitant so an always-refuse
implementation cannot pass vacuously. The first packet includes, where
applicable:

- duplicate and reordered delivery with byte-equal effective projection,
  retained distinct semantic variants/required receipts, and no requirement
  to retain each byte-identical transport retry;
- same proposed origin key with conflicting content/fidelity;
- current-URI hydration substituted for an admitted CID;
- a bridge post attempting heterogeneous cluster union;
- a later applicable adverse basis shadowed by older permissive evidence, plus
  an irrelevant/invalid newer wrapper that must not shadow a valid basis;
- activity/admission/computation/generated time substituted for qualifying
  evidence time;
- incomplete coverage rendered as zero, singleton, complete, or global
  absence;
- a failed stage surrounded by successful stages;
- an omitted stage inventory, lost refusal, remapped refusal, duplicate
  location ID, and explanation/base-result disagreement;
- current eligibility advancing beyond a cached cluster generation;
- individually non-determining or differently budgeted surfaces whose
  retained, adaptive, cross-cutoff, or cross-role join reveals removed
  membership;
- a filed or popular contest treated as an override or revocation;
- a soft/event-hypothesis result altering hard membership or standing; and
- a stock-feed result presented as exact admitted-CID hydration.

Counterexamples are minimized and retained as regression fixtures. The packet
also pins which input perturbations were *not* tested and the operating regime
within which an invariance claim applies.

The suite includes a disconnected green stage and a bypass path that reaches a
published output without traversing one required edge. Local stage success is
insufficient in both cases.

## What remains human and governed

The following are not machine-dischargeable merely because a local model can
represent a selected policy:

- which trust/custody evidence is admissible;
- which normalization or relationship semantics are acceptable;
- which clocks or relations may refresh standing and which thresholds apply;
- privacy, retention, contest, abuse, and resource budgets;
- whether two posts concern the same event, a witness is independent, an
  update is stronger, or a claim is true, resolved, or safe;
- comprehension, coherence benefit, harm, and “feels like a real place”;
- adequacy of the abstraction for the deployed sociotechnical system; and
- migration, policy ratification, exposure advancement, OQ selection, BUILD
  authorization, and slice acceptance.

These appear as named human/governance obligations or open findings. A model
must not fill them with convenient defaults.
